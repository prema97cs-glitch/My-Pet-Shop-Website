Bully Kutta

Origin: India / Pakistan Border

Price: ₹20,000 – ₹50,000

Category: Guard Dog / Mastiff

Traits: Powerful, Dominant, Fearless

⭐ Special Features

  • Extremely strong and muscular build
  • Known as the "Beast from the East"
  • High guarding and territorial instincts
  • Requires a confident, experienced owner

📏 Physical Details

Size: Giant / Large

Build: Heavy Muscular

🏡 Care & Training

Needs intensive social training

Requires large open spaces

Not suitable for first-time owners

🍖 Recommended Food

High protein/High calorie diet for muscle maintenance:

  • Beef & Chicken
  • Eggs & Milk
  • Large-breed specific dry food

🏥 Health & Vaccination

Vaccinated: ✔️ Yes

Requires basic shots (Rabies, Parvo, Distemper). Known for being hardy, but watch for joint health in such a large breed.

❤️ Why Choose Bully Kutta?

For those seeking the ultimate guardian, the Bully Kutta is a symbol of raw power and unyielding loyalty. It is a true protector for large properties.
