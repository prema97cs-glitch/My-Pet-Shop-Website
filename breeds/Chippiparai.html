Chippiparai Dog

Origin: Tamil Nadu, India

Price: ₹10,000 – ₹20,000

Category: Hunting Dog

Best For: Active owners with open spaces

⭐ Special Features

  • Extremely fast runner and agile sighthound
  • Intelligent and quick to learn commands
  • Highly energetic and athletic build
  • Traditionally used for hunting and guarding

📏 Physical Details

Height: 24 – 28 inches

Weight: 15 – 20 kg

Lifespan: 12 – 15 years

🏡 Care & Maintenance

Needs high levels of daily exercise

Not recommended for small apartments

Thrives in warm, tropical climates

🍖 Recommended Food

Focus on light and healthy nutrition for stamina:

  • Fresh Chicken & Eggs
  • Rice and lean proteins
  • Balanced nutrient-rich meals
⚠️ Note: Avoid heavy, oily foods to maintain their lean body.

🏥 Health & Vaccination

Basic vaccinations are completed. Keep up with boosters for:

Rabies, Parvo, and Distemper.

Generally a very healthy breed, but regular vet check-ups are needed to track their fitness.

❤️ Why Choose Chippiparai?

The Chippiparai is the ultimate choice for an active lifestyle—a silent, swift, and loyal companion that represents the heritage of South India.
