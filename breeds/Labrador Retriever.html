Labrador Retriever

Origin: Canada / UK

Price: ₹20,000 – ₹50,000

Category: Family Dog

Traits: Friendly, Loyal, Playful

⭐ Special Features

  • Consistently ranked as the most popular pet dog worldwide
  • Extremely patient and gentle with children and other pets
  • Highly intelligent and eager to please, making training easy
  • Natural swimmers with a strong love for fetching and play

📏 Physical Details

Size: Medium to Large

Coat: Short, dense, and water-resistant fur

🏡 Care & Maintenance

Needs regular physical exercise to stay fit

Requires minimal grooming; simple brushing suffices

Highly social; thrives on human companionship

🍖 Recommended Food

Balanced nutrition to prevent weight gain:

  • High-quality dry kibble (Portion controlled)
  • Cooked lean meats and plenty of vegetables

❤️ Why Choose Labrador Retriever?

The Labrador is the ultimate family companion. If you want a loyal, happy, and affectionate dog that will be your best friend for life, the Lab is the perfect choice.
