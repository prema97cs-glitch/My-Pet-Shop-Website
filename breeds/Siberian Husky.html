Siberian Husky

Origin: Siberia (Russia)

Price: ₹50,000 – ₹1,00,000

Category: Working Dog

Traits: Energetic, Friendly, Intelligent

⭐ Special Features

  • Stunning wolf-like appearance with unique eye colors
  • High endurance and incredible running speed
  • Famous for a friendly and outgoing social nature
  • Possesses a thick double coat built for cold climates

📏 Physical Details

Build: Athletic, lean, and medium-sized

Coat: Thick double coat (requires grooming)

🏡 Care & Maintenance

Needs a strictly cold or air-conditioned climate

Requires very high daily physical activity

🍖 Recommended Food

High-energy diet to support an active lifestyle:

  • High-protein premium dry food
  • Fresh meat and healthy fats

❤️ Why Choose Siberian Husky?

The Husky is a beautiful, high-energy companion. If you live in a cool environment and love an active, outdoor lifestyle, the Husky is a loyal partner for your adventures.
