Kanni Dog

Origin: Tamil Nadu, India

Price: ₹12,000 – ₹25,000

Category: Guard Dog / Hunting Dog

Best For: Farm houses, Open spaces, Security

⭐ Special Features

  • Strong protective instinct for guarding
  • High-speed runner (traditionally used for hunting)
  • One-person dog: Extremely loyal to the owner
  • High natural immunity to local diseases

📏 Physical Details

Height: 25 – 30 inches

Weight: 16 – 22 kg

Lifespan: 10 – 12 years

🏡 Care & Maintenance

Needs daily exercise (very active)

Best suited for outdoor/open living

Minimal grooming needed

🍖 Recommended Food

A high-protein diet is essential for this active breed:

  • Boiled chicken & Eggs
  • Rice & Broth
  • Quality dry dog food
⚠️ Avoid: Spicy food, Oily food, and Excess salt.

🏥 Health & Vaccination

Basic vaccinations are already completed. Regular boosters required for:

Rabies, Parvo, and Distemper.

Strong natural immunity, but regular vet check-ups are recommended to keep them in peak shape.

❤️ Why Choose Kanni Dog?

Kanni is the perfect choice for a loyal, intelligent, and low-maintenance guard dog. Its speed and bravery make it a legendary Indian breed.
