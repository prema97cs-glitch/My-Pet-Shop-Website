German Shepherd

Origin: Germany

Price: ₹20,000 – ₹60,000

Category: Guard / Police Dog

Traits: Intelligent, Loyal, Brave

⭐ Special Features

  • World-renowned for police and military service
  • Highly trainable and eager to learn new tasks
  • Exceptional guardian for families and property
  • Possesses a sharp mind and courageous heart

📏 Physical Details

Size: Large working breed

Coat: Double coat (Standard or Long haired)

🏡 Care & Training

Needs consistent mental and physical work

Requires regular social interaction

Standard grooming for shedding control

🍖 Recommended Food

A high-performance diet to maintain energy and muscle:

  • High-protein premium dry food
  • Boiled Chicken and eggs
  • Supplements for joint and bone health

🏥 Health & Vaccination

Vaccinated: ✔️ Yes

Up to date on standard shots. It is important to monitor joint health and maintain a healthy weight to prevent hip and elbow issues.

❤️ Why Choose German Shepherd?

The German Shepherd is more than just a pet; it is a loyal partner. If you are looking for an intelligent, protective, and dedicated companion, this is the ultimate breed.
