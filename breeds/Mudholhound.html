Mudhol Hound

Origin: Karnataka, India

Price: ₹10,000 – ₹25,000

Category: Hunting Dog

Traits: Fast, Loyal, Alert

⭐ Special Features

  • Excellent runner with incredible speed
  • Traditionally used for hunting and guarding
  • Keen sense of sight and high endurance
  • Extremely loyal to its family

📏 Physical Details

Size: Large & Lean

Build: Aerodynamic athletic body

🏡 Care & Maintenance

Requires high levels of exercise

Best suited for open spaces or large yards

Low maintenance grooming

🍖 Recommended Food

A high-performance breed needs a protein-rich diet:

  • Boiled Chicken & Meat
  • Rice with Broth
  • High-protein commercial dog food

🏥 Health & Vaccination

Vaccinated: ✔️ Yes

Naturally hardy breed. Requires basic vaccinations (Rabies, DHLPP) and regular check-ups to maintain peak physical condition.

❤️ Why Choose Mudhol Hound?

The Mudhol Hound is a legendary Indian sighthound. If you are looking for a swift, courageous, and loyal companion that thrives on activity, this is the perfect breed.
