Rottweiler

Origin: Germany

Price: ₹30,000 – ₹80,000

Category: Guard Dog

Traits: Strong, Protective, Confident

⭐ Special Features

  • One of the most powerful and effective security dogs
  • Intense loyalty and devotion to their family
  • Natural protective instincts and very courageous
  • Extremely calm and steady when properly trained

📏 Physical Details

Size: Large and Muscular

Coat: Short, black with clearly defined tan markings

🏡 Care & Maintenance

Needs early socialization and firm training

Requires daily physical and mental exercise

Low maintenance coat; weekly brushing is enough

🍖 Recommended Food

Needs a high-quality diet to support large muscle mass:

  • Protein-rich premium dry kibble
  • Boiled meat and eggs
  • Calcium and joint supplements for bone health

❤️ Why Choose a Rottweiler?

The Rottweiler is a world-class guardian. If you are looking for a powerful, intelligent, and deeply loyal protector for your home and family, the "Rottie" is a perfect choice.
