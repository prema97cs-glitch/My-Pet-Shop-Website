Indian Spitz

Origin: India

Price: ₹5,000 – ₹15,000

Category: Companion Dog

Traits: Friendly, Active, Alert

⭐ Special Features

  • Excellent family dog, great with children
  • Extremely easy to train and eager to please
  • Very adaptable to apartment living
  • Alert watchdog that will bark to notify of strangers

📏 Physical Details

Size: Small to Medium

Coat: Thick, silky white fur

🏡 Care & Grooming

Needs regular brushing for the coat

Moderate exercise requirements

Very adaptable to local Indian climates

🍖 Recommended Food

Simple, healthy home-cooked or commercial diet:

  • Cooked Rice & Vegetables
  • Boiled Chicken
  • Balanced dry kibble

🏥 Health & Vaccination

Vaccinated: ✔️ Yes

Requires standard vaccinations. Known for being a very hardy and long-lived breed with fewer health issues than foreign breeds.

❤️ Why Choose Indian Spitz?

The Indian Spitz is a bundle of joy. If you want a playful, loyal, and intelligent companion that fits perfectly into an Indian home, this is the breed for you.
