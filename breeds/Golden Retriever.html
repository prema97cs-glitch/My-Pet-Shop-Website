Golden Retriever

Origin: Scotland

Price: ₹25,000 – ₹70,000

Category: Family Dog

Traits: Friendly, Gentle, Patient

⭐ Special Features

  • Widely considered the best family dog in the world
  • Extremely gentle and famously loves children
  • Highly intelligent and very easy to train
  • Excellent therapy and service dog potential

📏 Physical Details

Size: Large & Athletic

Coat: Beautiful, dense golden water-repellent coat

🏡 Care & Grooming

Needs regular brushing to manage shedding

Thrives on human companionship and play

Requires moderate daily exercise and mental stimulation

🍖 Recommended Food

Needs a well-balanced diet to maintain health and coat shine:

  • High-quality balanced dry kibble
  • Cooked vegetables and lean meats
  • Omega fatty acids for coat health

🏥 Health & Vaccination

Vaccinated: ✔️ Yes

Standard vaccinations completed. Regular check-ups are important, along with a healthy diet to avoid obesity and support joint health.

❤️ Why Choose Golden Retriever?

The Golden Retriever is pure sunshine. If you want a loyal, affectionate, and happy companion that will love every member of your family, this is the breed for you.
