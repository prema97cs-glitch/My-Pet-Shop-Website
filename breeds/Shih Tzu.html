Shih Tzu

Origin: Tibet / China

Price: ₹20,000 – ₹60,000

Category: Toy Dog

Traits: Cute, Friendly, Affectionate

⭐ Special Features

  • The perfect indoor lap dog for apartments
  • Known for a very friendly and happy personality
  • Minimal shedding (Hypoallergenic qualities)
  • Loves human companionship and attention

📏 Physical Details

Size: Small / Toy breed

Weight: 4 – 7 kg

🏡 Care & Maintenance

Requires daily grooming for its long coat

Low exercise needs; perfect for indoor living

🍖 Recommended Food

Balanced diet suitable for small breeds:

  • Small-kibble dry food for tiny jaws
  • Cooked vegetables and lean protein

❤️ Why Choose Shih Tzu?

The Shih Tzu is a little lion with a big heart. If you want a charming, gentle companion that fits perfectly into a modern indoor lifestyle, this is the breed for you.
