Beagle

Origin: United Kingdom

Price: ₹20,000 – ₹50,000

Category: Hunting Dog

Traits: Curious, Active, Friendly

⭐ Special Features

  • Possesses one of the best senses of smell among all dogs
  • Small, compact size makes them great for families
  • Incredibly curious and loves to explore their surroundings
  • Has a very musical bark and "bay" sound

📏 Physical Details

Size: Small to Medium

Coat: Short, easy-to-care-for tri-color fur

🏡 Care & Maintenance

Needs regular long walks to burn energy

Requires a secure yard as they love following scents

Very low grooming requirements

🍖 Recommended Food

They are prone to overeating, so a monitored diet is key:

  • Nutritious low-calorie dry kibble
  • Cooked vegetables (carrots and beans)
  • Avoid excessive treats to prevent weight gain

❤️ Why Choose a Beagle?

The Beagle is a bundle of energy and joy. If you want a small but sturdy companion who is always up for an adventure, the Beagle is the perfect addition to your home.
