Bakharwal Dog

Origin: Himalayan Region, India

Price: ₹15,000 – ₹30,000

Category: Guard Dog

Traits: Strong, Protective, Courageous

⭐ Special Features

  • Traditionally used to protect livestock from predators
  • Very powerful, heavy-boned, and muscular build
  • Naturally protective of its family and territory
  • Brave enough to face large wild animals

📏 Physical Details

Size: Giant / Large Breed

Coat: Very thick double coat for cold protection

🏡 Care & Maintenance

Thrives in cooler climates; needs shade in heat

Requires large open spaces to patrol

Needs regular grooming due to thick fur

🍖 Recommended Food

Requires high-calorie, nutritious meals for its size:

  • Fresh Meat and Bones
  • Cooked Grains and Rice
  • Large-breed specific protein diet

🏥 Health & Vaccination

Vaccinated: ✔️ Yes

A very robust and sturdy breed. Requires regular vaccinations (Rabies/DHLPP). Monitor for joint health due to their heavy build.

❤️ Why Choose Bakharwal Dog?

The Bakharwal is a fearless and majestic guardian. If you have the space and want a loyal, powerful protector that is part of India's mountain heritage, this is the breed for you.
