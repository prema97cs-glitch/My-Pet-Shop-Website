Rajapalayam Dog

Origin: Tamil Nadu, India

Price: ₹15,000 – ₹30,000

Category: Guard Dog

Best For: Security, Farm houses

⭐ Special Features

  • One of the best guard dogs in India
  • Highly territorial and protective nature
  • Strong muscular body with a majestic white coat
  • Extremely brave and loyal to the family

📏 Physical Details

Height: 25 – 30 inches

Weight: 18 – 25 kg

Lifespan: 10 – 12 years

🏡 Care & Maintenance

Needs plenty of space to roam

Daily exercise is mandatory

Low grooming requirements

🍖 Recommended Food

A balanced diet for a large guard dog:

  • Chicken & Mutton
  • Rice & Eggs
  • High-quality protein sources
⚠️ Avoid: Oily and spicy food.

🏥 Health & Vaccination

Basic vaccinations are completed. Ensure regular follow-ups for:

Rabies, Parvo, and Distemper.

Known for their stamina, but require regular check-ups to maintain bone health.

❤️ Why Choose Rajapalayam?

The Rajapalayam is a symbol of Indian pride—brave, strong, and a superior guardian for your home and farm.
