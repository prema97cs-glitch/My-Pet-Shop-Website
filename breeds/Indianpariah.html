Indian Pariah Dog

Origin: Indian Subcontinent (Native)

Price: ₹2,000 – ₹10,000

Category: Native / Heritage Breed

Traits: Intelligent, Healthy, Adaptable

⭐ Special Features

  • One of the most intelligent and alert dog breeds
  • Extremely high natural immunity to local diseases
  • Very low maintenance and highly adaptable
  • Deeply loyal and protective of their families

📏 Physical Details

Build: Lean, athletic, and sturdy

Coat: Short, easy-to-clean fur

🏡 Care & Maintenance

Minimal grooming required

Thrives in almost any living condition

Highly energetic; loves daily walks

🍖 Recommended Food

Thrives on a variety of simple, nutritious diets:

  • Simple home-cooked food (Rice, Curd, Vegetables)
  • Boiled Chicken or Fish
  • Very flexible with commercial dog food

🏥 Health & Vaccination

Health Status: ✔️ Naturally Strong

While they have superior natural immunity, regular basic vaccinations (Rabies/Parvo) are still required to keep them and your family safe.

❤️ Why Choose Indian Pariah?

Choosing an Indian Pariah means choosing a piece of history. They are the most resilient, loyal, and low-maintenance companions you could ever ask for.
