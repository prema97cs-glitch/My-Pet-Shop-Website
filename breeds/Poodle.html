Poodle

Origin: Germany / France

Price: ₹30,000 – ₹80,000

Category: Companion Dog

Traits: Very Intelligent, Alert, Active

⭐ Special Features

  • Ranked as one of the most intelligent dog breeds in the world
  • Hypoallergenic coat which is excellent for allergy sufferers
  • Extremely versatile and excels in agility and obedience
  • Highly elegant appearance with a sharp, quick mind

📏 Physical Details

Size: Available in Toy, Miniature, and Standard

Coat: Dense, curly, and non-shedding fur

🏡 Care & Training

Requires regular professional grooming

Needs daily mental stimulation and puzzles

Highly social and loves being part of family activities

🍖 Recommended Food

A balanced diet to support their active minds and bodies:

  • Premium dry kibble tailored to their size
  • Boiled lean meats and fresh vegetables
  • Small, frequent meals for Toy and Miniature varieties

🏥 Health & Vaccination

Vaccinated: ✔️ Yes

Requires standard vaccinations and regular ear cleanings. They are generally long-lived but need consistent dental care.

❤️ Why Choose Poodle?

The Poodle is a brilliant companion. If you want a dog that is easy to train, hypoallergenic, and incredibly smart, this elegant breed is the perfect choice.
